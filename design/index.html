Design

I use content design—structure, layout, and interaction—to help users access the right level of information at the right moment, balancing clarity, engagement, and necessary detail.

Help Center rebrand, navigation, and Admin experience

Problem: The Help Center needed to reflect Dashlane’s new visual identity and give visitors, especially business admins, clearer ways to find the right content.

Solution: I partnered with a designer to implement the rebrand across the site’s HTML and CSS. We refined typography and interactions and added an AI quick summary and zoomable article images. In the next phase, I led the technical implementation of a sidebar table of contents, audience badges, and a distinct Admin experience within a highly customized Vue component.

Impact: The rebrand and navigation improvements received positive feedback from senior leaders and the creative team. After the new navigation launched, visits to the Admin page increased by more than 300%.

I worked with design and CX content partners on the UX decisions, tested the experience thoroughly, and coordinated the rollout.

Automated Help Center video library

Problem: Support videos were scattered, making them difficult for customers and internal teams to find.

Solution: I scripted a Vimeo metadata feed and built a library page with member-type filters, in-page video playback, and links to related articles. I collaborated with the video specialist to refine the experience.

Impact: The library launched as a centralized resource for customers, Support, Customer Success, and Sales. Later updates improved playback, localization, sharing, and downloads.

Help Center reorganization and search readiness

Problem: The Help Center structure needed to serve visitors better and be readable by search and AI crawlers.

Solution: I reorganized the Help Center around the processes customers need to complete, making the content easier to follow and easier for AI search tools to understand and surface (GEO).

Details: I built an internal tool to map and share proposals, then planned and validated a new hierarchy of categories and sections. I separated broad articles into focused guides, created a navigation mirror that works without JavaScript, and designed GA4 click tracking to measure the impact.

Impact: Crawled pages rose from 3,380 to 4,280 (about 27%) during the week the navigation mirror launched.
